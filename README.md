# 🏛️ 3D Museum of Memories & Birthday Celebration (Sister's Birthday Edition)

An interactive 3D virtual art gallery & birthday celebration website built with **Three.js**, **GSAP**, and **Web Audio API**.

---

## ✨ Features & Storyline

### 📸 4 Interactive 3D Photo Exhibits (Your Real Memories):
1. **Exhibit #1: Little Steps, Pure Smiles** *(Childhood Days)*
   - Holding hands as toddlers with her flower garland crown and matching peach/orange outfits.
2. **Exhibit #2: Growing Up Side-by-Side** *(School & Youth)*
   - Standing stylishly together in her green/red traditional churidar and your sharp red suit coat.
3. **Exhibit #3: Bond That Lasts Forever** *(Present Day)*
   - Your candid selfie together in front of the temple steps.
4. **Exhibit #4: Sweet Memories & Celebrations** *(Sweet Sister Moment)*
   - Sister feeding brother cake/sweets at home, sharing smiles and love.

---

### 🎂 The Grand Finale: 3D Birthday Cake & Candle Ceremony
- At the end of the museum gallery (at the glowing circular marble altar):
- A **tiered 3D Birthday Cake** decorated with pink frosting, pearl piping, and cherries **smoothly rises up from the pedestal**.
- **5 golden candles automatically ignite** with real-time flickering flames and warm candlelight illumination!
- An interactive prompt appears:
  > **"Make a Secret Wish! 🕯️"**  
  > *"The candles are lit! Close your eyes, make your deepest wish, and blow them out!"*
- When she clicks **"💨 Blow Out the Candles!"**:
  - Realistic candle blowing sound effect plays.
  - The flames extinguish.
  - Multi-stage **confetti cannons and fireworks explode** across the screen with a harp chime sound!
  - A heartfelt, glowing **Warm Birthday Wish Letter from her brother** appears with emotional words and blessings!
  - She can then view and release glowing 3D sky lanterns into the celestial sanctuary ceiling.
